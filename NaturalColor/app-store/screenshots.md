# Screenshot and image direction

Use the final app build on a supported iPhone. Export actual screenshots at an accepted iPhone display size; check the current App Store Connect size requirements when uploading. Never stretch a smaller screenshot. Simulator interface previews are QA evidence, not a photographed scene or proof of camera behaviour.

1. Camera open, Simple mode. A quiet architectural detail or plant in late daylight; a small heading, “Make room.” Keep actual controls legible.
2. A finished image filling most of the page. “Keep the moment.” Choose a daylight image with visible texture and no identifying faces, receipts or number plates. Show the app’s real output without extra grading.
3. Camera controls, HEIC Medium and RAW off. “Ready for everyday.” Let the settings communicate the defaults.
4. Review with compare and exposure controls. “A lighter touch.” Use the same image as the camera example; no exaggerated before/after.
5. Advanced view. “When you want more.” Actual exposure readings and grid; avoid any brand-camera resemblance in props, typefaces or hardware imagery.

Visual system: near-black, warm off-white and the app’s muted amber accent; generous margins, small restrained headings, photographs first. Keep the independent generic lens icon. No camera-maker names, product marks, vendor colour-system claims, emulation promises or borrowed product imagery.

User-supplied assessment photos remain private. Select and approve publication-ready images before including any in a public repository or App Store listing. Do not use recognisable people as marketing images without appropriate permission. The current draft package deliberately includes no tester photographs.
