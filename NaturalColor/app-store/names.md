# Name shortlist

Working title remains Natural until the publisher chooses the release name.

| Name | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| **Heldlight** | Recommended. Photographic, tactile and memorable; suggests keeping a moment. |
| **Daymere** | Soft and open; works with a restrained wordmark and daylight imagery. |
| **Stillfold** | Quiet, editorial; suggests the photograph as something worth keeping. |
| **Lumenry** | A more overt connection to light, with a concise distinctive spelling. |
| **Mereframe** | Unadorned and photographic; a little more literal. |

Public Apple software searches in Australia and the United States on 17 October 2026 returned no exact listings for these names. This does not establish App Store Connect reservation availability or trademark clearance. Check the selected name before finalising or publishing it. Daymere also returned a similarly spelled Daymera result in the US search, which warrants attention if selected.

Keep the icon generic and independently authored. Align the final name across the app display, camera wordmark, About, permission/recovery copy, App Store Connect localised title, screenshots and published support/privacy pages. Keep the existing technical bundle identifier for updates.
