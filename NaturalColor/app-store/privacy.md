# Privacy

The camera and editor process photographs on your device. The app has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or app-operated photo upload service. It does not collect personal data for the developer.

Camera access is used to take photographs. Add-only Photos access is used to save the photographs you choose to create. The system photo picker shares only the items you select. Files imports use the system document picker.

The app stores your camera and export preferences on the device. Temporary working copies support editing. Captures and developed copies are kept in the app’s Files folder if saving to Photos fails, so you can recover them. Existing originals from earlier versions are retained. You can remove app documents in Files or by deleting the app.

Photos saved to your library may sync through services you enable, such as iCloud Photos. Sharing a file uses the destination you select and that service’s privacy practices.

For a support/privacy request, use the publisher’s support page. The publisher must publish this policy with a working support contact before App Store submission.
