# Using the camera

The app opens to Simple mode. Tap the viewfinder to focus, choose a lens and press the shutter. Allow Photos access when asked. A finished photo saves to your library; you can keep shooting while it saves.

Open Camera controls with the sliders button. HEIC / Medium / RAW off are the defaults. JPEG offers broader compatibility. High and Small change compression, while native pixel dimensions stay intact. If you select a standard aspect ratio, the finished photo is cropped.

Turn on Save RAW original for a separate RAW and finished photo on supported cameras. RAW files can be much larger. The setting is unavailable when the selected camera does not support RAW.

Tap the thumbnail to review, compare with the original or adjust the finish and exposure. Camera returns to shooting. Use Import for a selected library photo or a file.

If saving fails, open Files → On My iPhone → Natural → Pending Captures and Processed. Preserve these copies before deleting the app. You can restore Photos access in iPhone Settings. Existing Originals from an earlier app version are left in place.
