# Naming directions

The installed working name remains Natural. No final release name has been selected.

## Poetic names about light and keeping a photograph

| Name | Intended meaning | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heldlight | Holding onto the light of a moment. | Photographic and tactile, but noticeably constructed. |
| Daymere | A coined blend of day and mere, a lake or pool. | Soft daylight and calm; the photographic connection needs explanation. |
| Stillfold | A still image and the idea of keeping a print. | Editorial and tactile, but abstract. |
| Lumenry | An invented name built from lumen, a measure of light. | Light-related, but can sound like a lighting or technology business. |
| Mereframe | Just the frame, with little fuss around it. | Understated; “mere” can also undersell the camera. |

This first group leans toward invented lifestyle-brand language. The newer direction is more grounded in the act of looking.

## Ordinary words about attention and recording

| Name | Idea | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Particular** | Paying attention to a specific detail, person or place. | Preferred direction: precise, editorial and observant. |
| Regarding | Looking closely; giving something your attention. | Thoughtful and open-ended. |
| An Aside | A small moment outside the main event. | Informal, personal and suited to everyday photographs. |
| Register | Light registering on a sensor; making a record. | More instrumental and camera-like. |
| Ordinary | Everyday life as a subject worth photographing. | Approachable and unpretentious; visual identity must convey quality. |

Particular is the strongest new direction; An Aside is the more personal alternative, and Register is the more functional alternative. These are recommendations to consider, not a decision to rename the app.

## Preliminary screening

Public Apple software searches in Australia and the United States on 17 October 2026 returned no exact listings for either table’s names. Similar uses exist, including Particular Augmented Reality, Aside-branded apps and Ordinary Store. Daymere also returned a similarly spelled Daymera result in the US search. These searches do not establish App Store Connect reservation availability or trademark clearance. Check the selected name before adopting or publishing it.

Proof and Take One had exact published matches; In Passing, Offhand and Counterpart had close existing app-name uses, so they were not advanced in the new shortlist.

Align the chosen name across the app display, wordmark, About, permission/recovery copy, App Store Connect localised title, screenshots and support/privacy pages. Keep the existing technical bundle identifier for updates. No vendor-camera branding or colour-system association should be introduced.
