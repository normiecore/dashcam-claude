## Description

A little light on the table. Someone looking up. The walk home.

Open straight to the camera, choose your frame and take the photo. Simple mode keeps the essentials close: focus, lens and shutter. Each shot saves directly to Photos.

Keep the camera’s native framing, or choose a familiar crop. Medium-quality HEIC keeps everyday files manageable without resizing the captured image. Prefer JPEG or a different compression setting? Change it in Camera controls.

For more control, turn on Advanced mode to see live exposure readings, adjust exposure compensation and add a composition grid. On supported cameras, save a RAW original alongside the finished photo.

Bring in a photo from your library or Files to adjust exposure and finishing, compare with the original, then save or share. Edits are processed on your iPhone. No account is required.

RAW support and maximum capture dimensions depend on the device and selected camera. Standard aspect ratios crop the finished image. JPEG is used if HEIC encoding is unavailable.
